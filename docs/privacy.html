Papir

Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 August 2026

Papir does not collect your data. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no account to create. Nothing you type into the app is sent to us, because there is no server to send it to.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — invoices, stock counts, contacts, movement history and the PDFs the app generates — is stored on your device.

The developer cannot see any of it.

iCloud

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, your invoices and stock are copied into your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

That data is held by Apple under your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to it and no ability to read, export or delete it.

What leaves your device, and only when you ask

  • PDF invoices — when you tap Share, iOS hands the file to whatever app you pick. Papir does not upload it anywhere.
  • Excel exports and JSON backups — written to a temporary folder and offered to the iOS share sheet. Where they go from there is your choice.
  • The Documents folder — generated PDFs are visible in the iOS Files app under Papir, so you can move or delete them yourself.

Tracking

Papir contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no crash reporters and no advertising identifiers. It does not use the Advertising Identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

Papir is a business tool and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. If iCloud sync was on, you can remove the app's iCloud data from iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
