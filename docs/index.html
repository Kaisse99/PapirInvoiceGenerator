A look inside

01

Everything is one swipe from here

The home screen is four directions and nothing else. Up writes an invoice, down opens the ones you have already written, left is the shelf, right is what sold. No tab bar to squint at, nothing nested three menus deep.

02

Write the row, watch the shelf

A row is a model, how many packs, how many pieces in a pack, and the price. Add the colours and they carry the packs between them, so the quantity can never disagree with the breakdown. What you actually have shows next to every colour while you type, so you find out you are short before you promise it.

03

The document your customer gets

Save, and Papir draws an A4 PDF: your name, theirs, every row with its colours and counts, the total. English, Ukrainian, Russian or Polish, and the document can be in a different language from the app, because the person reading it often is.

04

Drafts and shipped, never mixed

Everything you have written, newest first, with its total and where it stands. A draft is still a promise; shipped means the packs have left the shelf. Search by customer or by model, or filter down to one kind.

05

The shelf keeps itself

Count packs by model and by colour. Mark an invoice shipped and exactly those packs come off; pull it back to draft and they go straight back on. A colour running low says so in red, and one that has run out says so louder.

06

What actually sold

Money by month, your most profitable models with the colours that carried them, and the customers worth keeping. Shipped invoices only, because a draft is not money yet and a screen that pretended otherwise would teach you to distrust every number on it.

What it does

Invoices in four languages

Rows with a model, colours, quantity and price. Save, and Papir renders an A4 PDF in English, Ukrainian, Russian or Polish. The document can be in a different language from the app.

Stock that keeps itself

Count packs by model and by colour. Mark an invoice shipped and those packs come off automatically. Pull it back to draft and they go straight back on.

An honest ledger

Every movement in or out is written down, so a number can always explain how it got there. Selling more than the shelf says is allowed, and turns red until you recount.

What actually sold

Money by month, the most profitable models, and your best customers. Shipped invoices only — a draft is a promise, not money.

An address book

Who you ship to, with city, address and phone. Pick a customer and their details print under their name on the document.

Your data, exportable

One Excel workbook for your accountant, or a full backup you can restore. Generated PDFs sit in the Files app where you can reach them.

Nothing leaves your phone

No account. No subscription. No analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no third-party code of any kind. Everything you type stays on your iPhone.

Turn on iCloud and your invoices sync to your own private iCloud database, which nobody else can read — not even the developer, who has no server to read it from. Read the privacy policy; it is short, because there is little to say.
