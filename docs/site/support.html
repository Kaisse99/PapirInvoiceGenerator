Getting around

Everything starts on the home screen, and every screen is one swipe away:

  • Swipe up — write a new invoice
  • Swipe down — your invoices
  • Swipe left — stock
  • Swipe right — statistics

The gear in the corner opens Settings.

Common questions

An invoice will not let me edit it.
Shipped invoices are locked, because the packs have already come off the shelf and the record has to keep matching the document. Return it to draft first; the stock goes back exactly as it was taken.

A colour count went negative.
That is on purpose. Selling more than the shelf says is always allowed, so a miscount never blocks a real order. The number turns red until you recount.

The price filled itself in.
Naming a model that has a price on the stock screen fills that price into the row. Type over it if this order is different — the field turns amber to say it disagrees with the shelf, which is a note, not an error.

My PDF is missing.
Open the invoice and tap Re-generate. The PDF is rebuilt from the rows, so nothing is lost. PDFs also live in the iOS Files app under Papir.

Can I get my data out?
Yes, two ways, both in Settings. Export everything writes one Excel workbook — invoices, stock, contacts and history — for reading and for your accountant. Backup writes a single JSON file that carries everything, including the links the spreadsheet flattens, and can be restored.

iCloud sync

Sync is off until you turn it on in Settings, and it needs a restart to take effect, because the database is opened one way or the other when the app launches.

With it on, invoices and stock appear on every device signed in to the same Apple Account. Your data goes into your own private iCloud database — nobody else can see it, including the developer.

If Settings says iCloud is unavailable, check that you are signed in to iCloud on the device and that iCloud Drive is on.

Keep a backup

Until iCloud is on, everything lives on this phone and nowhere else. If the phone is lost, so are the invoices. Take a backup from Settings now and again and keep the file somewhere else — Files, Drive, or email it to yourself.

Languages

The app speaks English, Ukrainian, Russian and Polish, set in Settings independently of your phone's language. Each invoice PDF can be generated in any of the four, so the document can be in a different language from the app.
