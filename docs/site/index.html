Papir

Invoices & Inventory

For people who sell boxes of things

Write an invoice, hand over a clean A4 PDF, and watch the packs come off the shelf as you ship. One app instead of a notebook and a spreadsheet that disagree with each other.

What it does

  • Invoices — rows, colours, prices, and an A4 PDF in English, Ukrainian, Russian or Polish.
  • Stock — a count per model and per colour, in whole packs, that moves by itself when an invoice ships.
  • Statistics — what actually sold, by month, by model and by customer. Shipped invoices only, never drafts.
  • Address book — who you ship to, filled into an invoice and printed under their name.

Your data is yours

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone, or in your own iCloud if you turn sync on. Export to Excel or take a full backup whenever you like.
